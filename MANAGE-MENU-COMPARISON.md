## Business settings detail

- Branding: brand/contact/address, logo/favicon, primary/contrast/navigation/CTA colors, invoice details, social links, about text, trust indicators, signature. Account timezone, week start, calendar grouping, footer and indexing controls. Tracking needs a messaging provider.
- Travel zones: name, minimum/maximum distance, miles/km, origin address, none/flat/distance fee, free distance and round trip, package scope. Automatic route-distance lookup requires a mapping integration.
- Tax zones: name, state restrictions, flat rate or automatic venue-based rate lookup (Check Cherry uses TaxJar). No automatic provider connection is assumed for EventDesk.
- Lead forms: ordered fields with label/width/required/optional/hidden, package/service/staff selections and custom questions, default form, confirmation text/redirect/booking-page action, submit button, CAPTCHA; configurable lead sources/types/event types and related-lead archiving.
- Proposal presets: theme, cover image, pricing title, alternatives layout, review/gallery limits, package ordering; About, Featured Gallery and Reviews submenus.
- Mini sessions: default terms and background. Dedicated session checkout is a separate booking workflow.
- Availability: business/service/group/package/extra shared capacity, available weekdays, notice/window, day vs scheduled time, inventory rules, simulation, staff settings and change policy.
- Places: address, public/private notes, preview/gallery/attachments, proposal-cover option and preferred packages.
- Referrals: enable tracking, staff/client page visibility, title/body. No reward payments are authorized automatically.
- Third-party integrations: licensed design libraries, analytics/chat, gallery/contact sync, SMS, appointments, accounting and API. Show accurate connection states; do not pretend accounts are connected.

## Accepted product decisions

- Keep EventDesk and independent business workspaces.
- Online submissions require owner approval.
- Message delivery remains off; drafts and review are available.
- Retain the site's current owner-only hosting audience.
- Payment-provider choice is being requested while the independent Manage work continues.

## Implemented in this update

All 16 Manage destinations are present. Package Manager remains the existing implementation. The new resource managers include active/archive filters, search, category or collection organization, image and document uploads, ordering, duplication, deletion, atomic bulk edits, and package restrictions.

- Add-ons and backdrops: quantities, hourly/daily/unit prices, included units, lead time, category defaults, gallery visibility, and booking extensions flow into estimates and scheduling. Quotes snapshot their prices and rules.
- Discounts and flex pricing: scoped fixed/percentage adjustments, date/weekday/location conditions, unique codes, and atomic redemption caps. Shared package capacity is checked in time choices and again during confirmation.
- Templates: original starter messages, draft previews and review queue, attachments, configurable triggers and conditions. Typed questionnaires, conditional questions, uploads, finalization/reopening and synchronization preserve existing answers. Checklist due dates and assignments flow into event planning and Sales tasks.
- Designs and users: collections, media, scope, profiles, role filters and saved preferences. Staff login permissions are explicitly not granted by a profile checkbox.
- Booking engine: presets, client and internal extra questions, extra-category selections, unavailable notices and privacy consent. Public lead forms validate submissions, prevent duplicate retries, and keep uploads readable only by the business owner.
- Payments: editable custom methods accepted by the payment ledger, invoice instructions, due-date/deposit terminology, and applied monthly/equal-installment/full-payment schedules. No online processor is connected.
- Business settings: branding, terms, travel/tax rules, lead forms, proposal themes/gallery/reviews, media, places and shared package availability.
- Website integration: working general/package/service booking links, lead forms and add-on/backdrop/design/staff galleries, iframe snippets, configurable galleries, copy and QR. Refer Friends offers a shareable EventDesk link and draft, with no invented reward promise.

## Explicit remaining differences and integration dependencies

This is not a claim of complete Check Cherry parity. Provider onboarding, online checkout and automatic charges await the provider decision and credentials. Email/SMS sending is intentionally deferred. Staff/customer authentication, invitations and their permission enforcement, e-signatures, external appointment and mini-session checkout widgets, CAPTCHA-provider verification, licensed design libraries, route-distance lookup, TaxJar and other third-party connectors are not connected. The independent availability-calendar widget and per-extra inventory pools are not implemented; booking links enforce the implemented package availability rules. Referral sharing does not yet track reward attribution. Advanced rich-text HTML, questionnaire timeline exports, and Check Cherry's hosted system templates are not reproduced.

The site remains owner-only. Enabling customer access is a separate access-policy change.
