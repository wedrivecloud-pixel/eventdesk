## Important behavior

- All new records and receipt objects are scoped to the authenticated business. Public booking responses never include sales notes, drafts, tasks, expenses, receipt metadata or financial history.
- New proposals require packages. Confirming still validates pricing/scheduling, required staff, notice, blackouts and business capacity. Online submissions remain requests for approval.
- Canceling/postponing/archiving/spam/trash are reversible lifecycle changes, preserving quotes and payments. Restoring a previously confirmed record returns it to proposal status to avoid silently reserving capacity.
- Regular sales records use version checks for edits. Expense CSV imports validate the entire batch before writing. Generated recurring expenses have deterministic occurrence IDs to prevent duplicates.
- Tax and profit/loss report labels describe the data actually recorded. Payment reports include financial history attached to trashed records; active booking totals exclude those records.
